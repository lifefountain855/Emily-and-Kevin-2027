Create a beautiful, modern, mobile-friendly static wedding website with an elegant and romantic design. The website should feel polished, easy to navigate, and optimized for both desktop and mobile devices. Use soft typography, high-quality imagery, subtle animations, and a cohesive color palette inspired by the couple's wedding theme.

## Website Structure

Create the following pages:

### 1. Landing / Welcome Page

The homepage should serve as a warm introduction to the wedding.

Include:

* Large hero image of the couple
* Couple's names prominently displayed
* Wedding date
* Short welcome message
* Countdown timer to the wedding day
* Navigation menu linking to all pages
* Call-to-action buttons for "View Event Details" and "RSVP"

Suggested content placeholders:

* Couple Names: Emily Hansen & Kevin Sapp
* Wedding Date: 1/25/2027
* Welcome Message: "We're so excited to celebrate with you and can't wait to share our special day."

---

### 2. Event Information Page

Provide all logistics and planning information guests need.

Include:

#### Ring Ceremony

* Ceremony start time
* Full venue name
* Complete address
* Embedded map

#### Reception

* Reception start time
* Full venue name
* Complete address
* Embedded map

#### Weekend Timeline

Display in an attractive timeline format:

* Sealing
* Welcome Gathering
* Ceremony
* Reception
* Dinner
* Dancing

Example format:

* 3:30 PM – Guest Arrival
* 4:00 PM – Ceremony
* 5:00 PM – Cocktail Hour
* 6:00 PM – Reception & Dinner
* 8:00 PM – Dancing
* 11:00 PM – Send-Off

#### Dress Code

Display attire expectations clearly.

Examples:

* Colored ties
* Formal
* Cocktail Attire
* Garden Party

Include practical recommendations such as:

* Outdoor ceremony footwear suggestions
* Seasonal weather considerations
* Jacket or shawl recommendations if applicable

#### Travel Information

Include:

* Nearest airports
* Airport names and distances
* Hotel recommendations with direct booking links
* Room block information if available

#### Transportation

Clearly explain available transportation.

Examples:

* No guest transportation provided
* Limited public transportation available
* Ride-share services recommended
* Parking information

### 3. Our Story Page

Sections:

#### How We Met

### 4. RSVP Page

Include:

Display RSVP deadline prominently.

Include a confirmation message after submission.

Suggested heading:

#### Groomsmen

Include:

### 6. FAQ Page

Create a comprehensive FAQ section using expandable accordions.

Include questions such as:

#### Are children invited?

Example answer:
"While we adore your little ones, the sealing and ring ceremony will be adults-only. Reception, everyone is invited."

#### What should I wear?

Reference the dress code and practical recommendations.

#### Is parking available?

Provide details about venue parking and any costs.

#### What airports should I fly into?

List nearby airport options.

#### Will transportation be provided?

Explain transportation availability.

#### Can I bring a plus one?

Provide the couple's policy.

#### What time should I arrive?

Recommend arrival time before the ceremony.

#### What if I have dietary restrictions?

Explain how guests can communicate accommodations.

#### Where should we stay?

Link to recommended hotels and room blocks.

#### How do I RSVP?

Direct guests to the RSVP page and embedded form.
